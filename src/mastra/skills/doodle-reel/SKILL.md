# Doodle Reel

Film a NEW shot starring a character the user already has doodles of. The
references define who the character IS — hair, face, colours, outfit — not the
composition of the shot.

## When this is the right skill

Pick this when the request describes something that is not already in the
picture:

- "make a video of her riding a scooter"
- "a clip of this character waving from a beach"
- "put my doodle in a coffee shop, five seconds"

Choose `doodle-motion` instead when the user wants THAT exact drawing to move
("animate this", "make the one you just made wave"). Getting this backwards
produces a technically fine clip of the wrong thing, which is the most common
mistake with this model.

## What to film

- One action, described plainly. Walks in and waves. Spins once. Sips coffee.
- One continuous camera move — a gentle hand-held drift or a slow push in.
  Not both, and never a whip pan or an orbit.
- Staging that survives a phone screen: full body or waist up, generous empty
  space, one flat background, at most two doodled props.

## How to run it

1. Gather the reference URLs. Best case is several doodles of the same
   character from this conversation; one is enough. Put the clearest full view
   first — references are cited in order.
2. Call `generateVideo` with `skill: "reel"`, those URLs as `referenceUrls`,
   and the user's action in `description`. If they named a length, pass
   `seconds` — otherwise it renders 5 seconds at 1 credit per second.
3. If the user has no doodle of the character yet, make one first with an image
   skill, show it, then offer the reel.
4. The tool returns `status: "queued"` with a `jobId`: the clip is RENDERING.
   Say it will land here on its own in about a minute and that it has sound,
   then stop.

## What not to do

- Do NOT re-call the tool for the same request, or call it to check progress.
  The finished clip is delivered to the app on its own.
- Do NOT send more than ten references — the extras are dropped, and the first
  ones are the ones that shape the character anyway.
- Do NOT describe or evaluate the clip. You have not seen it.
- Do NOT film a real, named person. This skill is for the user's own doodle
  characters.
