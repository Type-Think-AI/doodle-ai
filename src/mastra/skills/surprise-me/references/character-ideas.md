# Character seeds

Mix one entry from two or three columns to invent a character when the user
gives no direction. Combining unrelated seeds produces more interesting
results than picking a single archetype.

| Hair                     | Vibe                | Signature detail            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Blunt bob, ink black     | Sleepy and content  | Oversized scarf             |
| Wild curls, copper       | Chaotic good        | Mismatched earrings         |
| Long braid, silver       | Quietly confident   | Round wire glasses          |
| Shaved sides, mint green | Playful troublemaker| Headphones around the neck  |

## Palette starters

- Warm cream background, terracotta and sage
- Soft white background, dusty pink and navy
- Warm white background, mustard and teal
- Pale grey background, lilac and burnt orange

## Guidance

- Give the character one clear point of visual interest, not five.
- Expression carries more personality than costume detail — commit to it.
- Asymmetry is a feature here: uneven linework and off-center framing are
  part of the naive style, not mistakes to correct.
- Keep the background clean regardless of how busy the character is.
