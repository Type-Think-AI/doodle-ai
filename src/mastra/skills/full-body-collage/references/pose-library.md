# Full-body action pose library

Six-panel sets that read well as a page. Every pose must show the complete
figure head to feet.

## Default mix

When the user has no specific request, pick six poses that vary energy and
direction so the page has rhythm:

1. Mid-jump, arms up, both feet off the ground
2. Walking toward the viewer, mid-stride
3. Dancing, hips turned, one arm raised
4. Standing still, hands in pockets, relaxed
5. Spinning or turning, clothing and hair trailing
6. Stretching or reaching upward

## Motion overlays

Match the overlay to the action so the marks explain the movement:

- arcs trailing kicks, spins, and swinging arms
- speed lines behind running and jumping figures
- small dust puffs at the feet on landings and stomps
- curved dashes showing the path of a spin

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Cropping any panel at the knees or waist (this defeats the skill)
- Six poses that all face the same direction with the same energy
- Changing the outfit between panels
- Static, symmetrical standing poses in more than one panel
