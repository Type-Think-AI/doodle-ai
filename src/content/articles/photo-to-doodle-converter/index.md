## Inside the conversion

The converter interprets your photo and rebuilds it in the house doodle style: heavy outlines, simplified shapes, flat color, warm-white ground. It keeps the parts that make the subject recognizable and lets go of texture, which is the whole reason a doodle looks hand-drawn instead of photocopied.

## Feeding the converter well

One clearly lit subject, facing the camera, plus a short prompt:

> Convert to a doodle. Keep the afro and the gold hoops. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

Named features guide the converter better than long descriptions.
