**Direct answer:** To turn a photo into a cartoon at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a short prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). It returns one square hand-drawn cartoon per run for 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts. The main hub is [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/).

The composer is on this page, so you can follow the steps right here.

## Step 1 — Choose a clear photo

One well-lit person or pet, facing the camera, eyes visible. Clean input means fewer tries to a keeper.

## Step 2 — Write a short prompt

In the box above, name a feature or two:

> Turn this photo into a cartoon. Keep the glasses and the ponytail. Flat color, warm-white background, no text.

## Step 3 — Sign in and attach

Sign in with Google, then attach your photo. Signing in lets the tool run the generation and track your credits.

## Step 4 — Generate and refine

Generate. One credit is reserved and a square cartoon returns in the house style — bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background. If a detail drifts, reply with a fix and go again.
