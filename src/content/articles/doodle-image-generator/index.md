**Direct answer:** The doodle image generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) makes one square hand-drawn image from a photo you upload. Sign in with Google, attach a picture, type your prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each image costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits to start.

The word "image" here is literal and small on purpose: you get a single finished picture, not a gallery grid or a batch export. That keeps the whole experience honest — one prompt, one photo, one image you can download and use.

## Anatomy of the output image

The file is a 1:1 PNG built for avatars, stickers, and posts. Style-wise it's the doodle house look: thick confident outlines, simplified features, flat color fills, and a clean warm-white background. The generator preserves identity cues — hair, glasses, expression — while shedding photographic detail.

![Square doodle image of a person with bold outlines on a warm-white background](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/generated/image_6397c145-1063-406c-b44a-49416cc92322.png)

## Prompting for a better image

Use one clearly lit subject, then keep instructions short:

> Doodle image of me. Keep the beard and denim jacket. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

Two or three named features steer the result far better than a paragraph of description.

## Questions people ask

**What image does the generator output?** One square PNG in a doodle style via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) — a single still, not a grid, print, or clip. Attach a photo and sign in first.

**Is the doodle image generator free?** Free to browse. Generating spends credits: free starter credits, 1 per image, refunded on failure. No subscription.

**Do I have to upload an image to start?** Yes for Normal. For a text-invented image, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**Can I use the image commercially?** You own what you generate, but only upload photos you have the right to use.

**Why did I get a slightly different image than last time?** Generation varies. Name the drifting feature in your next prompt and generate again.
