## What to look for

- **Photo fidelity.** The result should still read as the person. A good generator preserves hair, glasses, and expression while simplifying; a bad one produces a generic face.
- **Honest pricing.** Clear credit costs, no paywall ambush, and refunds on failed runs. If you can't tell what a generation costs, that is a red flag.
- **Output you can steer.** Short prompts should change the result in predictable ways. If prompts do nothing, you are using a filter, not a generator.
- **A defined scope.** Knowing what a tool won't do — no video, no prints here — is as valuable as its feature list.
