**Direct answer:** Doodle art at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) is a hand-drawn illustration made from your photo. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each piece is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts.

Doodle art is the playful, notebook-margin look — confident lines, flat color, nothing fussy. Here it comes from your photo, so the art feels personal rather than generic.

## The style, and how to steer it

The Normal skill keeps identifying features — hair, glasses, expression — and simplifies the rest into flat, cheerful art. A short prompt guides it:

> Doodle art of me. Keep the curls and the round glasses. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

## Questions people ask

**What is doodle art here?** A loose hand-drawn style from a photo via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Is making it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Do I need to draw anything myself?** No — you attach and describe; the skill draws.

**Can I make art without a photo?** Normal needs a photo; for text-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**What can I do with the art?** Use the square image as an avatar, post, or sticker.
