**Direct answer:** The random doodle generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) maps to the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/): sign in and generate, and it invents a random fictional hand-drawn doodle character — no photo and no prompt required. Each doodle is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Sometimes you do not want to upload a face or think of a prompt — you just want a surprise. This generator gives you exactly that: hit generate and get a fresh fictional doodle character every time.

## Randomness, hand-drawn

The [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) picks a random character description — hairstyle, expression, accessories — and draws it in the same naive doodle style as the rest of the studio. It is the only skill that works with nothing attached.

## Steer it, or don't

- **Fully random:** leave the box empty, sign in, and generate.
- **Nudged:** add a hint — "a cozy winter character," "something spooky," "a beach vibe" — and it stays surprising within your theme.

## Generate

Type an optional hint in the box above, sign in with Google, and generate. One credit per doodle, refunded on failure. Keep generating for a fresh character each time.

**How does it work?** It maps to [Surprise](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/), which invents a random fictional doodle character and draws it. No photo or detailed prompt needed.
