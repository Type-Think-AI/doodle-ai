**Direct answer:** The doodle idea generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns a spark into a drawing — the Surprise skill invents a doodle from your words, no photo. Sign in with Google, type your idea in the box above, and run the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/). Each doodle is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts.

When you're stuck for what to draw, the fastest cure is seeing something. This generator collapses brainstorm and sketch into one move: type a rough idea, get a doodle, and let it point you to the next one.

## Questions people ask

**How does the idea generator help?** It draws your spark as a doodle via the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) — brainstorm and sketch in one. Sign in first.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**What if I don't have an idea yet?** Start with anything concrete and generate; one doodle sparks the next.

**Does the same idea always draw the same doodle?** No — it reinterprets each run.

**Can it draw an idea based on my photo?** This is the no-photo Surprise skill; for a real face use the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/).
