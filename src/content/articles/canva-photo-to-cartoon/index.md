## Two different shapes of tool

Canva is a full design suite — layouts, text, templates, and a cartoon effect among many features. Doodle AI does one thing: it converts a photo into a hand-drawn cartoon doodle through a chat composer. If your goal is the cartoon itself, a single-purpose converter is direct. If your goal is a whole poster or post with the cartoon inside it, a design suite handles the layout part.

These aren't mutually exclusive. Generate the cartoon here, download the square image, and drop it into whatever design tool you already use — Canva included — as a normal asset. The converter handles the drawing; the design suite handles the composition.
