## What conversion means here

The converter reads your photo and rebuilds it in the doodle style: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, clean warm-white background. It holds onto identity cues — hairstyle, glasses, expression — and drops fine texture, which is exactly the difference between a doodle and a traced photocopy.
