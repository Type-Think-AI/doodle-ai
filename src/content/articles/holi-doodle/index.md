**Direct answer:** To make a Holi doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in, attach your photo, and run the [Festival skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/festival/) with "Holi" in your prompt. You get a festive hand-drawn portrait of yourself splashed with Holi color. It does **not** draw deities or religious figures. Each run is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Holi is the festival of color, and a "Holi doodle" is a fun way to mark it: a hand-drawn portrait of you covered in playful color bursts, made from an ordinary photo. You do not need an already-messy picture — the doodle adds the color for you.

## A festive Holi portrait

The [Festival skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/festival/) redraws **your photo** into a hand-drawn festive portrait. For Holi it layers bright color splashes and celebratory energy around your own likeness, keeping your face, hair, and expression recognizable. It is a portrait of a person, not an image of any deity or religious icon.

## How to make one

Type "Holi" plus a short note in the box above, sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, and generate. A starter prompt:

> Make a festive Holi doodle from my photo. Keep my hair and face. Bright color splashes around me, cheerful mood, no text, no deities.

One credit per run, refunded on failure.
