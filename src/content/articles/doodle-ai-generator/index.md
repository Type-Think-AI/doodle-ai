**Direct answer:** The Doodle AI generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) is a chat-first tool that turns a photo into a hand-drawn doodle with AI. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type your request in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each doodle costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Doodle AI is a studio you talk to. Instead of sliders and menus, you write what you want and attach a photo, and the AI answers with a drawing. That conversational shape is the point — refining a doodle feels like asking a friend to tweak a sketch, not wrestling with a control panel.

## The chat-first workflow

Everything happens in one thread. You describe the doodle, attach the reference photo, and the AI returns a square image. Don't love a detail? Reply with the fix and it draws again. Each generation is its own credit, and failed runs refund so you are never charged for nothing.

## A prompt to open with

> Doodle of me from this photo. Keep the round glasses and the beanie. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

Short and specific beats long and vague. Two or three named features give the AI something to hold.
