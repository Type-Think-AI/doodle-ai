**Direct answer:** Doodle sketch at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns your photo into a quick, sketchy hand-drawn doodle. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each sketch is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts.

A sketch is the fast, rough version — looser lines, less polish, more energy. Ask the Normal skill for that feel and it leans sketchier while still keeping your recognizable features.

## Questions people ask

**What is a doodle sketch here?** A quick, sketchy take on your photo via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**How do I get a sketchier feel?** Ask for 'loose sketchy outlines, minimal color'.

**Can I sketch without a photo?** Normal needs a photo; for text-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**How is a sketch different from a full doodle?** Same engine; 'sketch' just emphasizes a looser line.
