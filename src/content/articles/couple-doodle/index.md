**Direct answer:** To make a couple doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in, attach a clear photo of two people together, and run the [Couple skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/couple/). You get one hand-drawn doodle of the pair. Download it for an anniversary post or a shared DP. Each run is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

"Couple doodle" is a favorite for anniversaries, Valentine's posts, and matching display pictures — and in India the related "couple cartoon" intent is huge. This page covers both: one photo of two people, one hand-drawn doodle of the two of you.

## What the couple skill draws

The [Couple skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/couple/) takes **one photo of two people** and returns **one hand-drawn doodle** of the pair in the naive marker-and-ink style: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color. It keeps each person's recognizable landmarks and draws them together in one frame.

![Hand-drawn couple doodle of two people together](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/edited/image_5ef46520-6769-496f-9f10-35863fac3234.png)

## A photo the doodle can read

- Both faces visible and reasonably close together.
- Even light — a window beats a dim restaurant.
- Avoid shots where one face is turned fully away or lost in shadow.
- If you want specific outfits or a festival look, name them in the prompt.

## Make one

Type an optional prompt in the box above, sign in with Google, attach the couple photo, and generate. A starter prompt:

> Make a couple doodle from this photo. Keep both hairstyles and our outfits. Warm background, no text.

One credit per run, refunded on failure. It works from a phone browser, so a camera-roll couple picture is fine.
