## From casual picture to clean doodle

The converter reads your picture and rebuilds the subject in the doodle style: heavy outlines, simplified shapes, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps the recognizable bits — hair, glasses, expression — and simplifies the rest, so even a quick phone picture becomes a tidy drawing.

## Picking a picture

One clear subject, decent light, facing the camera, then a short prompt:

> Convert this picture to a doodle. Keep the hoodie and the curly hair. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

Crop out extra people or clutter first for a sharper result.
