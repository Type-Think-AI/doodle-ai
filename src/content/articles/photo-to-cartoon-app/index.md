**Direct answer:** Looking for a photo to cartoon app? [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) is a web app — it runs in your phone or desktop browser with no install. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each cartoon is 1 credit, refunded on failure, and new accounts get free starter credits. Main hub: [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/).

Most "app" searches want the convenience of tapping something on a phone. This delivers that without the app store: the browser is the app, so there is nothing to download or update.

## App-like, install-free

Open the page and the composer is ready. Attach a photo, prompt, generate — the Normal skill returns a hand-drawn cartoon doodle in the house style: bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps the features that make the subject recognizable.
