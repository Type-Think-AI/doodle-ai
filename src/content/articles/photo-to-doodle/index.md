**Direct answer:** To convert a photo to a doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in, attach a clear face-forward photo, type what you want in the box above, and run the [Normal doodle skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). You get one square hand-drawn doodle — not a filter, a 3D render, or a video. Each conversion reserves 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts start with free starter credits.

"Photo to doodle" is the exact-match phrase for one simple job: you have a picture, and you want it redrawn in a loose, cheerful, hand-drawn style you can keep and share. This page is the converter for that job. It is not a whiteboard-video tool, not a stock-doodle library, and not a text-to-sketch generator. It starts from your photo.

## What a photo-to-doodle conversion produces

The Normal skill takes **one uploaded photo** and returns **one 1:1 doodle**. The house style is a naive marker-and-ink look: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, and a clean warm-white background. The model is instructed to keep the parts that make the picture recognizable — hairstyle, face shape, expression, skin tone, glasses, and accessories — then translate them into illustration rather than trace them photographically.

![Square hand-drawn doodle avatar with bold outlines on a warm-white background](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/generated/image_6397c145-1063-406c-b44a-49416cc92322.png)

That is different from a photo filter that stays glued to every pixel of the original. A doodle simplifies. It throws away detail on purpose. If you wanted a photoreal touch-up, this is the wrong converter — try a retouch tool. If you wanted a doodle that still reads as you, this is exactly it.

## How to use the converter

1. Type a short prompt in the box at the top of this page. It pins the Normal skill and carries your words into the studio.
2. Sign in with Google. Browsing is open, but converting needs an account.
3. Attach one clear photo in the composer.
4. Generate. One credit is reserved, and a square doodle comes back.
5. Download it, or ask for a refinement in the same thread.

A first prompt you can paste:

> Turn this photo into a doodle. Keep my glasses and the hoodie color. Closed-mouth smile, clean warm-white background, no text.

Short prompts that name two or three landmarks work better than long costume lists. The photo already carries most of the information; your job is to point at what matters.

## What it is not

- **Not a video.** There is no timeline, no animation, no lip-sync. You get a still.
- **Not a print.** No shipped posters or mugs. Download the digital file and print it yourself if you want.
- **Not a stock library.** Every result is generated from your photo, not pulled from a clip-art bin.
- **Not guaranteed identity.** Two runs can drift. Judge each doodle on its own.

## Questions people ask

**How does the photo to doodle converter work?** You attach one photo and the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) redraws it as a square hand-drawn doodle. It keeps landmarks like hair, glasses, and expression rather than applying a photo filter. Sign in first; a photo is required.

**Is the tool free?** Reading is free. Converting uses credits: new accounts get free starter credits, each doodle costs 1 credit, and failed generations refund. There is no paywall.

**What kind of photo works best?** One person filling the frame, face toward the camera, even light, eyes visible. Group shots, blur, and very dark photos make the model guess.

**Can I convert a picture that is not a face?** Normal is built around a person or pet subject, not a scene tracer. For a printable line drawing, use [Photo to Coloring Page](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-coloring-page/).

**Does it store my photo?** It is uploaded through Doodle AI’s server-owned PicX connection to generate the doodle and hosted along that path. Only upload photos you have the right to use.

**Will the doodle look like me?** It keeps landmarks but does not guarantee identical results. If a detail drifts, name it in your next prompt and generate again.
