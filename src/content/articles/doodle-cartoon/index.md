**Direct answer:** A doodle cartoon at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) is a hand-drawn image with cartoon energy and doodle looseness, made from your photo. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each one is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts. The main hub is [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/).

"Doodle cartoon" flips the emphasis of cartoon doodle — a touch more cartoon bounce, still with the friendly hand-drawn line. Either way, it starts from your photo and keeps the subject recognizable.

## The look, and how to steer it

The Normal skill preserves features like hair, glasses, and expression, then simplifies the rest into a lively cartoon doodle. Guide it briefly:

> Doodle cartoon of me. Keep the fade and the round glasses. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.
