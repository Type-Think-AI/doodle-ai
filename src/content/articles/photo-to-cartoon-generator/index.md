**Direct answer:** The photo to cartoon generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) redraws your photo as a square hand-drawn cartoon. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each cartoon costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits. For the full walkthrough, see the [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/) hub.

"Generator" here means one clean transform: photo in, cartoon out. No batch grid, no export settings — a single finished picture per run.

## What the generator makes

It reads the subject in your photo and rebuilds it in the house cartoon-doodle style: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. Identity cues carry over; texture is simplified away, which is what makes it read as an illustration rather than a filtered photo.

## Prompting the generator

> Cartoon of me. Keep the ponytail and the hoop earrings. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.
