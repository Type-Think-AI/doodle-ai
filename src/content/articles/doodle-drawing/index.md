**Direct answer:** Doodle drawing at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns your photo into a loose, hand-drawn doodle. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each drawing is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts.

A doodle drawing is the casual sketch you'd make in the margins if you could draw. This does the pen work for you, from a real photo, so the drawing still looks like the subject.

## The look, and steering it

The Normal skill keeps recognizable features and simplifies the rest into a loose drawing. A short prompt helps:

> Doodle drawing of me. Loose outlines, flat color. Keep the beard and cap. No text, warm-white background.

## Questions people ask

**What is a doodle drawing here?** A loose hand-drawn rendering of your photo via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Do I draw it myself?** No — you attach and describe; the skill draws.

**Can I make a drawing without a photo?** Normal needs a photo; for text-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**Can I change it after?** Yes — reply with a tweak and draw again; each is a 1-credit run.
