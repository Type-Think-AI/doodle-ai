**Direct answer:** To make a doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). You get one square hand-drawn doodle per run for 1 credit, refunded on failure, and new accounts start with free starter credits.

"Make a doodle" is about as plain a request as it gets, and the tool keeps it that way. There's no canvas to learn and no menu to master — you point at a photo, say a few words, and a doodle comes back. That is the whole loop.

## The simplest way to make one

Making a doodle is really three moves: attach a photo, describe it briefly, generate. The Normal skill turns your photo into a square doodle in the house style — bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background — keeping the features that make the subject recognizable.

![A doodle made from a photo, bold outlines on a warm-white background](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/generated/image_6397c145-1063-406c-b44a-49416cc92322.png)

## A prompt to make your first one

> Make a doodle of me. Keep the glasses and the short hair. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

Short and specific works best. Name a couple of features and let the model handle the rest.

## Questions people ask

**How do I make a doodle here?** Type, sign in, attach a photo, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). It redraws the photo as a square doodle.

**Does it cost anything?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Can I make one without a photo?** Normal needs a photo; for idea-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**How many can I make?** As many as your credits allow — one per run, failed runs refund.

**Can I make changes after the first?** Yes — reply in the thread and make another; each is a 1-credit run.
