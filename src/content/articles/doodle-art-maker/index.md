**Direct answer:** The doodle art maker at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) makes hand-drawn doodle art from a photo. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type what you want in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each piece costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts begin with free starter credits.

"Maker" tools usually hand you a blank canvas and wish you luck. This one is different — it does the making. You supply the reference and the direction; it supplies the linework. That suits anyone who has a clear idea but no drawing skill.

## What gets made

One square piece of doodle art per run. The maker leans into a playful, notebook-margin style: heavy outlines, simple shapes, flat color, warm-white ground. It keeps the identifying bits of your subject and simplifies everything else, which is exactly what makes it feel drawn rather than filtered.

## How to steer the maker

Attach one well-lit subject and give a compact prompt:

> Make doodle art of me. Keep the short curls and the striped shirt. Flat color, warm-white background, no text.

Naming a couple of signature details does more than a wall of description.

## Questions people ask

**What is the doodle art maker built to do?** It makes one square piece of doodle art from an attached photo via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first; it is not a manual canvas.

**How much does it cost to make doodle art?** Free to look, credits to make: free starter credits, 1 per piece, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Can I make art without a photo?** Normal needs a photo. For text-invented art, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**Is this a drawing canvas?** No — you describe and attach; the maker generates. It is not a paint app.

**Can I redo a piece I don't like?** Yes — adjust and regenerate. Each attempt is a 1-credit run, refunded if it fails.
