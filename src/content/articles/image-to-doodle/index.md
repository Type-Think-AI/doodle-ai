People search "image to doodle" when the source is not a fresh camera photo. It might be an image saved from a chat, an exported portrait, or a picture already sitting in a folder. ## Image in, doodle out

The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) redraws **one image** into **one 1:1 doodle** with bold outlines, simplified features, and flat color on a warm-white background. It keeps the landmarks that make the subject recognizable and translates them into illustration. Because it simplifies rather than traces, a clean image with a clear subject converts far better than a cluttered one.

## Which images convert well

**Convert cleanly**

- A single person or pet filling most of the frame.
- An exported portrait with even lighting.
- A saved image where the face and hair read clearly.

**Recrop or skip**

- Screenshots of screenshots, or memes with caption text baked in.
- Group images where several faces share a small area.
- Very dark or motion-blurred images.
- Images that already have stickers or overlays on them.

## Questions people ask

**How is image to doodle different from photo to doodle?** Same job — one hand-drawn doodle from a picture — but "image" covers files you did not shoot: a saved image, an exported portrait, a scan. The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) still needs a clear single subject.

**Can I convert a screenshot?** You can upload one, but screenshots of screenshots and captioned memes make a poor doodle. A clean single-subject image works far better.

**Is it free?** Browsing is free. Each conversion is 1 credit, new accounts get free starter credits, and failures refund. No paid checkout today.

**What formats can I upload?** Standard image files up to 20 MB. Empty and non-image files are rejected.

**Do I have to sign in?** Yes — uploading and generating need Google sign-in, which grants your free starter credits.

**Can I convert an image I found online?** Only images you have the right to use. Do not convert someone else’s photo, artwork, or a trademarked image without permission.
