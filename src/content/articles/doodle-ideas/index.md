## Ideas, then a doodle

The [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) draws a fictional doodle character from an idea with no upload. That is what makes this a working idea generator rather than a listicle.

## Doodle ideas by theme

**Cozy:** a cat curled on a stack of books; a mug of cocoa with a tiny face; a sleepy fox in a scarf.

**Food:** a dumpling with legs; a proud avocado; a stack of pancakes wearing a crown.

**Space:** a sleepy astronaut cat; a planet with sunglasses; a rocket held together with tape.

**Animals:** a duck in rain boots; a hedgehog holding a leaf umbrella; a whale wearing a party hat.

**Seasonal:** a snowman melting into a puddle-smile; a pumpkin with a cheeky grin; a spring bird on a bud.

Pick one, tweak it, and generate. The recipe: **subject + one trait + a style word** like cute or bold.
