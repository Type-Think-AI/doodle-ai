## What the AI keeps and drops

The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) redraws **one photo** into **one 1:1 doodle**. The AI is instructed to keep hairstyle, face shape, expression, skin tone, glasses, and distinctive clothing, then simplify everything into a naive marker-and-ink style on a warm-white background. It drops fine skin texture, background clutter, and photoreal shading — that is the point of a doodle.

## AI vs a filter app

A photo filter layers an effect on top of the original pixels. This generator throws pixels away and redraws. That is why the result reads as a drawing rather than a processed photo. If you wanted a photoreal touch-up, use a retouch tool; if you wanted a hand-drawn doodle that still reads as you, use this.
