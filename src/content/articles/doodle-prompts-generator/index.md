**Direct answer:** The doodle prompts generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) draws a fictional doodle from a written prompt — no photo. Sign in with Google, type your prompt in the box above, and run the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/). Each generation is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts.

This is the prompt-to-picture loop in one place: write the prompt, get the doodle, refine the prompt, go again. The Surprise skill invents the subject, so all you supply is the words.

## Writing prompts that steer well

> A curious fox holding a lantern in a forest, gentle. Doodle style, flat color, warm-white background, no text.

Concrete subject, one or two features, a mood. Iterate by changing a single element at a time.

## Questions people ask

**How does it work?** You write a prompt and the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) draws a fictional doodle — no photo. Sign in and type it.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**How specific should my prompt be?** Concrete but short — subject, a feature or two, a mood.

**Can I iterate?** Yes — tweak and generate again; each is a 1-credit run.

**Does it need a photo?** No — Surprise invents from text; for a real face use the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/).
