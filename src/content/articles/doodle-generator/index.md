**Direct answer:** The doodle generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns a photo into one square hand-drawn doodle. Sign in, attach a picture, type an optional style note in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). It leads with your photo, not with text-to-sketch. Each generation is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Search results for "doodle generator" are a mix of random text-to-sketch toys and pattern makers. This one is different on purpose: it starts from a real picture, so the doodle actually looks like the subject instead of a generic squiggle. If you want a fictional character with no upload, that fork exists too — but the default here is photo-first.

## What the generator makes

The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) produces **one 1:1 doodle per run**: bold marker-and-ink outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps the landmarks that make the subject recognizable — hair, face shape, expression, glasses, clothing — and redraws them by hand rather than filtering the photo.

## Photo-first, with a light text touch

You *can* type a style note — "thicker outline," "warmer paper," "more chibi" — and it will guide the look. But the photo carries the likeness. A short prompt plus a good picture beats a long text description with no picture. If you truly have no photo and do not want to upload one, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) instead; it invents a fictional doodle character.

## Run it

1. Type an optional prompt in the box above (it pins the skill).
2. Sign in with Google.
3. Attach one clear picture.
4. Generate — 1 credit, one doodle.
5. Download or refine.

A starter prompt:

> Generate a doodle from this photo. Keep my hair and glasses. Clean warm-white background, no text.

**What does this doodle generator do?** Turns a photo into one square hand-drawn doodle via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/), with bold outlines and flat color. Photo-first, not text-only.

**Can I generate without a photo?** Yes, with [Surprise](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/). The Normal generator expects a photo.
