**Direct answer:** The doodle art generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns a photo into hand-drawn doodle art. Sign in, attach a photo, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). "Doodle art" here means a naive marker-and-ink illustration with bold outlines and flat color. Each piece is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Doodle art is deliberately loose and cheerful — the opposite of photoreal. This generator makes that art from your photo, keeping the parts that make you recognizable.

## Questions people ask

**What is doodle art here?** A naive marker-and-ink illustration — bold outlines, simplified features, flat color — generated from your photo by the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/).

**Is it free?** Browsing is free. Each piece is 1 credit, new accounts get free starter credits, failures refund. No paid checkout.

**From an idea?** Yes — [Surprise](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) draws fictional doodle art from a prompt.

**Can I sell it?** You keep the rights you have in your photo; review the terms before commercial use and check each piece.

**What style?** A consistent house doodle style, nudged by words like cute or bold. Not a multi-style catalog.
