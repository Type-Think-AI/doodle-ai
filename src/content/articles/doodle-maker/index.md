**Direct answer:** The doodle maker at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns a photo into one square hand-drawn doodle image. Sign in, attach a picture, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). This is a **still-image** maker — not a whiteboard-video tool. Each doodle is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

The phrase "doodle maker" is crowded with whiteboard-animation software that draws explainer videos. This is not that. This maker produces a single hand-drawn doodle *image* from your photo. If you came looking for animated whiteboard videos, this is the wrong tool and we will not pretend otherwise.

## A still-image doodle maker

The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) makes **one 1:1 doodle** from **one photo**: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps recognizable landmarks — hair, face shape, expression, glasses, clothing — and redraws them by hand.

## What it is not

- **Not whiteboard video.** No animated hand drawing your slides.
- **Not a timeline editor.** One image per run.
- **Not a print shop.** Digital file only; print it yourself.

## Make one

Type an optional prompt in the box above, sign in with Google, attach a photo, and generate. A starter prompt:

> Make a doodle from this photo. Keep the hair and glasses. Warm-white background, no text.

One credit per doodle, refunded on failure. Works in the phone browser too.
