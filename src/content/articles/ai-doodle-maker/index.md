**Direct answer:** The AI doodle maker at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) uses a generative model to turn a photo into one square hand-drawn doodle. Sign in, attach a photo, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). It is a still-image maker, not a video tool. Each doodle is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

"AI doodle maker" describes the method — the doodle is generated by a model, not traced by a filter. The Normal skill keeps your recognizable features and redraws them in a naive marker-and-ink style.

## How the AI makes a doodle

![Square AI-drawn doodle from a photo](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/generated/image_6397c145-1063-406c-b44a-49416cc92322.png)

Attach a photo, add an optional style note, and generate. The model simplifies detail on purpose so the result reads as a drawing. A starter prompt:

> Make an AI doodle from this photo. Keep my hair and glasses. Warm-white background, no text.

## Questions people ask

**What is an AI doodle maker?** A tool that uses a generative model to make a doodle from a photo. The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) does it — one photo in, one square doodle out.

**Is it free?** Browsing is free. Each doodle is 1 credit, new accounts get free starter credits, failures refund. No paid checkout.

**Need a photo?** Normal needs one; [Surprise](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/) works with no upload.

**Animated doodles?** No — still image only.

**Will it look like the photo?** It keeps landmarks but does not guarantee identical results.
