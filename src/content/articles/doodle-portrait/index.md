**Direct answer:** To make a doodle portrait at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in, attach a face-forward photo, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). You get one square hand-drawn portrait that keeps your face, hair, and expression in a naive marker-and-ink style. Each run is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

A "doodle portrait" is a portrait framing of the same hand-drawn doodle: one person, drawn as a bust, with the face carrying the likeness. It is illustrated character, not a photoreal headshot.

## What a doodle portrait is

The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) redraws **one photo** into **one 1:1 portrait doodle**: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps hair, face shape, expression, skin tone, glasses, and accessories, then translates them into illustration.

## Prompt for a portrait

> Draw a doodle portrait from this photo. Keep my hair, glasses, and expression. Bust framing, clean warm-white background, no text.

Tighter framing on the face carries more likeness. Name two or three landmarks rather than a long list.

## Portrait vs headshot

A doodle portrait simplifies and exaggerates for character. It is not a photoreal professional headshot. If you need a corporate photo, use a photographer; if you want a hand-drawn portrait with personality, use this.

## Questions people ask

**What is a doodle portrait?** A hand-drawn illustrated portrait from a photo. The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) draws one square portrait keeping your features.

**Different from a headshot?** Yes — stylized illustration, not a photoreal professional photo.

**Is it free?** Browsing is free. Each portrait is 1 credit, new accounts get free starter credits, failures refund. No paid checkout.

**Best photo?** Face-forward, even light, visible eyes, tight framing.

**Two people?** Use [Couple Doodle](https://doodleai.art/couple-doodle/). This is single-subject.

**Will it look like me?** It keeps landmarks but does not guarantee identical results. Name a feature if it drifts.
