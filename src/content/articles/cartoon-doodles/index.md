**Direct answer:** Cartoon doodles at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) are playful hand-drawn images made from your photo. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each one is 1 credit, refunded on failure, with free starter credits for new accounts. The main hub is [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/).

"Cartoon doodles" lives right where cartoon and doodle meet — the bounce of a cartoon with the loose, hand-drawn feel of a doodle. It comes from your photo, so it still reads as the subject.

## The style, and steering it

The Normal skill keeps recognizable features and simplifies the rest into a bouncy, flat-color cartoon doodle. A short prompt helps:

> Cartoon doodle of me. Keep the glasses and curly hair. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

## Questions people ask

**What are cartoon doodles here?** Playful images between a cartoon and a doodle, from a photo via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Are they free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Do they need a photo?** Normal does; for text-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**How is this different from the main cartoon page?** Same engine; the [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/) hub covers the full process.

**What do I get per run?** One square still.
