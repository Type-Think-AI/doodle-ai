**Direct answer:** To turn a photo into a doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a short prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). It returns one square hand-drawn doodle per run. Each run is 1 credit, refunded on failure, and new accounts start with free starter credits.

This is the short how-to. The composer is already on this page, so you can follow along in place — no separate tool to open.

## Step 1 — Pick a good photo

Choose one clearly lit person or pet, facing the camera, eyes visible. A clean photo gives the model the most to work with and gets you a keeper faster.

## Step 2 — Type your prompt

In the box above, write a short instruction that names a feature or two:

> Turn this photo into a doodle. Keep the glasses and the curly hair. Flat color, warm-white background, no text.

## Step 3 — Sign in and attach

Sign in with Google, then attach your photo in the composer. Signing in is what lets the tool run a generation and track your credits.

## Step 4 — Generate and refine

Generate. One credit is reserved and a square doodle returns in the house style — bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background. If a detail drifts, reply with a fix and generate again.

## Questions people ask

**What do I need before I start?** A clear photo of one subject and a Google account, plus the free starter credits every new account gets.

**How many steps does it take?** Four: prompt, sign in, attach, generate — one square doodle per run from the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/).

**How much does each doodle cost?** One credit, refunded on failure; free starter credits to begin.

**What if my first doodle isn't right?** Reply with a tweak and generate again; each attempt is a 1-credit run.

**Can I follow these steps without a photo?** These steps use Normal; without a photo, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).
