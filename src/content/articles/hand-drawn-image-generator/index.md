**Direct answer:** The hand-drawn image generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) redraws your photo with a loose, hand-drawn look. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each image costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

"Hand-drawn" is a style, and this generator is built to fake the human hand convincingly — the slight looseness, the confident-but-imperfect line, the flat color a marker leaves. It does that from your photo, so the image feels sketched by someone who knows the subject.

## What "hand-drawn" means in the output

The generator avoids the crisp, mechanical feel of a filter. It goes for the notebook-margin look: bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. It keeps the features that identify the subject and lets the rest go loose, which is exactly what a real hand would do.

## Prompting for the hand-drawn feel

Feed it one clearly lit subject and steer with words about line quality:

> Hand-drawn doodle of me. Loose marker outlines, flat color. Keep the glasses and beard. No text, warm-white background.
