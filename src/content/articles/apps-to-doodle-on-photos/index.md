**Direct answer:** If you are hunting for apps to doodle on photos, [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) is a browser tool that takes a different route — instead of letting you scribble lines over a photo, it redraws the whole photo as a hand-drawn doodle. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each doodle is 1 credit, refunded on failure, and new accounts get free starter credits.

There are two very different things people mean by "doodle on photos." One is a sketch app where you draw squiggles on top of a picture. The other is turning the picture itself into a doodle. This tool is the second kind, and it is honest about that up front.

## What this tool does instead of scribbling

Rather than hand you a brush, it interprets your photo and rebuilds it in the doodle style — bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background — keeping the features that identify the subject. No stylus skill required, because you are not drawing; the model is.

## Using it like a phone app, from the browser

Because it is web-based, your phone browser is the app. Attach a photo, prompt it, and generate:

> Doodle of this photo. Keep the cap and the freckles. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

**Do I draw on the photo myself?** No manual drawing — you describe and attach; the skill draws.

**Is it free like a phone app?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Does it work on my phone?** Yes — in the phone browser, nothing to download.

**Can it doodle on a photo with no face?** Normal centers on a person or pet; for outlines use [Photo to Coloring Page](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-coloring-page/).
