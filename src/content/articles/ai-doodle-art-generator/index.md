**Direct answer:** The AI doodle art generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) turns a real photo into one piece of square hand-drawn doodle art. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a short prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Most "art generator" tools spin something out of a text box. This one works the other way around: it starts from a face you already have and reinterprets it as playful ink-and-marker art. That single design choice — photo first — is what makes the output read as *you* rather than as a random stranger the model imagined.

## What "doodle art" means here

Doodle art is the loose, cheerful, notebook-margin look: chunky outlines, a few confident lines, flat blocks of color, and a clean warm-white background. The generator keeps the parts that identify a person — hairstyle, glasses, expression, skin tone — and drops the rest on purpose. Simplification is the point, not a bug.

## Getting a clean result

Feed it one person filling the frame, lit evenly, eyes visible. Then keep the prompt short and pointed:

> Doodle art of me. Keep the curly hair and hoop earrings. Warm-white background, no text.

Naming two or three landmarks beats listing a full wardrobe. The photo already carries the detail; your words just steer the emphasis.
