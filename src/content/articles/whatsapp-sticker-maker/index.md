## What the sticker maker makes

The [Sticker skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/stickers/) draws a **square sheet** of hand-drawn die-cut sticker doodles from **one photo** — several expressions or poses in the naive doodle style. You download the sheet and use the stickers as images.

## What it is not

- **Not a WhatsApp pack export.** No transparent, packaged sticker pack with one-tap add.
- **Not a print sheet.** No cut lines or print specs.
- **Not chat-app native.** You send the images through the chat yourself.

For a native transparent pack, ChatGPT Images and Apple's built-in tools handle chat-app sticker formats — a different product.
