## How AI reads the photo

The model doesn't paint over pixels. It recognizes a face, glasses, a hairstyle, a smile, and re-renders each in the doodle house style: bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background. The interpretive step is why the same photo can land two slightly different doodles — and why prompts actually steer the result.
