**Direct answer:** The image to doodle converter at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) redraws a saved image into a square hand-drawn doodle. Sign in with Google, attach the image, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each conversion costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

"Image" is a slightly broader word than "photo," and that is deliberate here — the file you convert doesn't have to come straight from a camera. A screenshot, a saved picture, a downloaded portrait: if it shows a clear person or pet, the converter can redraw it.

## What the converter does with an image

It reads the subject in your image and rebuilds it in the doodle style — bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. Identity cues carry over; texture and clutter get simplified away. The source being an "image" rather than a "photo" changes nothing about how the redraw works.

## Best images to feed it

One clear subject, facing forward, decent resolution, then a short prompt:

> Convert this image to a doodle. Keep the glasses and the ponytail. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

**What kind of image can I convert?** A clear image of a person or pet — screenshot, download, or gallery file — via the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Does image quality matter?** Yes — clear, well-lit, forward-facing converts best.

**Can I convert a logo or landscape?** No — Normal centers on a person or pet; for outlines use [Photo to Coloring Page](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-coloring-page/).

**How many doodles per image?** One per run; convert again for a different look.
