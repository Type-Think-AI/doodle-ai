**Direct answer:** Doodle color pages at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) are hand-drawn line-art doodles you color yourself. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, ask for clean line art in the box above, and run the crayon-based [skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/crayon/). Each page is 1 credit, refunded on failure, and new accounts get free starter credits.

A color page is just an outline waiting for crayons. This makes one from your photo — a person, a pet, a simple object — as a clean doodle outline rather than a filled illustration.

## Getting a clean outline

Prompt for openness:

> Line art coloring page from this photo. Bold outlines, minimal shading, white background, no text.

Simple, bold lines are easier to fill than fussy detail.
