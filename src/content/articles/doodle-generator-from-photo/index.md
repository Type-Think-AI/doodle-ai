**Direct answer:** The doodle generator from photo at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) starts with your picture, not a text box. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each doodle costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

The phrase "from photo" is the whole promise. This is not a generator that guesses a face out of nothing — it anchors on the picture you provide, so the doodle actually resembles the person. That is why the photo is required rather than optional.

## Why starting from a photo matters

A photo gives the model a real reference: this hairstyle, these glasses, this expression. It keeps those cues and translates them into the doodle style — bold outlines, flat color, warm-white background — instead of inventing a stranger. Simplification happens on the texture, not the identity.

![Doodle generated from a photo, bold outlines on a warm-white background](https://cdn.picxstudio.com/api/generated/image_6397c145-1063-406c-b44a-49416cc92322.png)

## Feeding it a good photo

One subject, even light, eyes visible, then a short prompt:

> Doodle from this photo. Keep the buzz cut and the round glasses. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.
