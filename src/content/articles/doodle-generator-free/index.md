## How the free part works

Two things are free without limit: reading these pages and signing in. Generating a doodle spends a credit, and your first credits are the free starter batch every new account gets. A run that fails is refunded, so you never burn a credit on a blank.

## Getting the most from your starter credits

Since each run costs a credit, a clear photo pays off. Use one well-lit subject and a short prompt:

> Doodle of me. Keep the glasses and the messy bun. Flat color, no text, warm-white background.

That gives you a keeper on fewer attempts, stretching the free credits further.
