**Direct answer:** The doodle drawing generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) takes a photo and returns a hand-drawn doodle drawing. Sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, type your request in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). One drawing costs 1 credit and refunds if it fails; new accounts begin with free starter credits.

Plenty of people can picture the doodle drawing they want but can't sketch it. That gap is the whole reason this generator exists. You bring the reference — your face, a friend, a pet — and it handles the pen work, producing the loose margin-of-the-notebook style without you ever touching a stylus.

## The drawing style it produces

Expect a single square image in a naive marker look: bold outlines, a handful of decisive strokes, flat color, warm-white background. The generator holds onto recognizable features — hair shape, glasses, expression — and lets go of fine texture. That is what separates a doodle drawing from a traced photocopy.

## Steering the pen

Give it a well-lit photo of one subject, then guide with a short prompt:

> Draw me as a doodle. Keep the ponytail and round glasses. Flat color, clean background, no lettering.

Adjectives about line weight ("chunky outline", "loose sketch") land more reliably than long backstories.

## Questions people ask

**How is a doodle drawing different from my photo?** A drawing simplifies. The [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/) reduces the photo to confident lines and flat color instead of copying pixels. Attach the photo and sign in first.

**Is the drawing generator free to use?** Browsing is free. Generating uses credits: free starter credits, then 1 credit per drawing, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Can I generate a drawing from imagination instead of a photo?** Normal draws from your photo. For a text-only drawing, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**How long does one drawing take?** A short wait per run, returning one square drawing. A stalled or failed run refunds the credit.

**Can I ask for changes to the drawing?** Yes — reply in the same thread and generate again. Each new drawing is its own 1-credit run.
