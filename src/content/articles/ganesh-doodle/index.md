This page is explicit about what it does and does not do. A "ganesh doodle" here means a festive celebration portrait of a person during the Ganesh Chaturthi season — a doodle of you in a celebratory mood. It does not draw the deity, an idol, or any religious artwork.

## A festive portrait of you, respectfully

The [Festival skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/festival/) redraws **your photo** into a hand-drawn festive portrait, keeping your face, hair, and expression recognizable and adding celebratory festival color and mood. To keep it respectful, it stays a portrait of a person and does not attempt any deity or idol imagery.
