**Direct answer:** To make an Onam doodle at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art), sign in, attach your photo, and run the [Festival skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/festival/) with "Onam" in your prompt. You get a festive hand-drawn portrait of yourself with pookalam-style floral color and festive mood. It does **not** draw deities or religious figures. Each run is 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits.

Onam is Kerala's harvest festival, rich with flowers, sadya, and color. An "Onam doodle" here is a hand-drawn portrait of you styled for the festival — floral pookalam-inspired color and a warm celebratory feel — made from an ordinary photo.

## A festive Onam portrait

The [Festival skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/festival/) redraws **your photo** into a hand-drawn festive portrait. For Onam it adds pookalam-style floral color and festive attire cues around your own likeness, keeping your face, hair, and expression recognizable. It is a portrait of a person, not an image of any deity.

## How to make one

Type "Onam" plus a short note in the box above, sign in with Google, attach a clear photo, and generate. A starter prompt:

> Make a festive Onam doodle from my photo. Keep my hair and outfit. Add floral pookalam-style color around me, warm festive mood, no text, no deities.

One credit per run, refunded on failure.
