**Direct answer:** The cartoon generator at [doodleai.art](https://doodleai.art) redraws your photo as a square hand-drawn cartoon. Sign in with Google, attach a photo, type a prompt in the box above, and run the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Each cartoon costs 1 credit and refunds on failure; new accounts get free starter credits. The main hub is [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/).

A cartoon generator should keep it simple: photo in, cartoon out, one at a time. That is what this is — a single clean transform, not a batch tool or an editor.

## What it generates

It reads your photo and rebuilds the subject in the house cartoon-doodle style — bold outlines, simplified features, flat color, warm-white background. Recognizable cues stay; texture is simplified away, so the result reads as an illustration.

## Questions people ask

**What does it need to run?** A photo, redrawn by the [Normal skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/normal/). Sign in first.

**Is it free?** Free browsing and free starter credits, then 1 credit each, refunded on failure. No paywall.

**Can it invent a cartoon from text?** Normal needs a photo; for text-only, use the [Surprise skill](https://doodleai.art/skills/surprise/).

**What does one run give me?** One square cartoon still.

**Where is the main page?** The [Photo to Cartoon](https://doodleai.art/photo-to-cartoon/) hub.
