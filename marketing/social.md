# Doodle AI Social Knowledge-Sharing Report

**Prepared:** 2026-08-25
**Project:** [Doodle AI](https://doodleai.art)
**Scope:** Live X posts, prompt-sharing behavior, and how people actually use cartoon/avatar/sticker tools
**Decision goal:** Fill the gap that SEO, AEO, ICP, and B2B research treated as a later “social distribution” bullet instead of a primary discovery channel.

> This report records what people currently share and copy on X. It is not a claim that Doodle AI is already part of those loops. No Doodle AI mention, citation, or official account activity was found in the live X searches below.

## 1. Executive decision

People do not discover photo-to-cartoon tools primarily by searching a generic brand. They copy a visible result, a prompt, or a one-click workflow from a post, then try to recreate it in the nearest tool that is already open: ChatGPT, Grok Imagine, Midjourney, or a linked playground.

Doodle AI should treat **shareable proof plus a copyable method** as the missing acquisition layer. The current product can generate the look. It does not yet participate in the loop that spreads that look.

### What live X evidence shows

1. **Hand-drawn doodle avatars are a live PFP trend on X**, independent of Doodle AI. On 2026-08-20, @ChapagainKushal posted that they saw many hand-drawn doodle avatars (citing @mannay and @kevin_t_ngo) and sent people to [lazyavatar.com](https://lazyavatar.com).
2. **Knowledge travels as prompts, not landing pages.** High-engagement posts share the exact prompt, a JSON breakdown of a reference image, “comment X and I’ll send the link,” or “DM me STICKER for the prompt.”
3. **ChatGPT shipped native stickers on 2026-08-24** with transparent backgrounds and iMessage/WhatsApp export. That is now the default consumer path for “turn a photo into a sticker.”
4. **Grok Imagine is the default cartoon/image path on X.** Public @grok replies tell users to paste a cartoon prompt into Grok Imagine. They do not name Doodle AI.
5. **Doodle AI has no measurable X presence.** Searches for `doodleai.art`, “Doodle AI,” and related product language returned no official posts and no user examples. An unrelated account `@doodleais` exists and is not this product.

### Highest-impact social move

Publish original before/after doodles with the prompt, the skill name, and a link people can actually use. Do this on an owned X account. Do not wait for dedicated `/photo-to-cartoon/` pages if the first public proof can ship from `/skills/normal/` today.

## 3. How people actually share knowledge

Observed formats, ranked by how often they appeared in the live sample:

| Format | What the poster does | Why it spreads | Doodle AI implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt dump | Paste the full prompt under a result image or video | Recipients can copy it into ChatGPT/Grok/Midjourney immediately | A Doodle result with no prompt is not reusable knowledge |
| “Comment/DM for the prompt” | Gate the method behind a reply or DM | Builds a list and creates a second touch | Useful later; do not lead with this before there is public proof |
| Before/after or multi-vibe grid | Show 1 photo → many styles | Proves likeness and range in one post | Collage, full-body, sticker sheet, and mood-caption skills already match this format |
| Tool roundup thread | List 5–10 avatar tools with links | High bookmarks; people treat it as a shopping list | Doodle AI is absent from these lists |
| Challenge / “drop yours” | Ask followers to post their result | Turns one post into a feed of UGC | Needs a public, fast first-result path |
| JSON-from-reference | Drop a Pinterest/X image into a model, extract a structured prompt, regenerate | Creators treat this as a “consistency trick” | Character save/@-mention is the product equivalent; it is not yet explained publicly |
| Account-inspired image | “Create an image inspired by my X account” | Uses PFP, banner, bio as identity | Adjacent trend; not Doodle AI’s photo-upload job |
| Animate a still | Use Grok Imagine 1.5 or similar to turn one avatar into a short clip | Video posts get more reach than stills | Do not promise video. A still doodle can still be the first frame someone animates elsewhere |

### Language people actually use

The live posts did **not** search for “doodle AI.” They used:

- “cartoon pfp”
- “hand-drawn avatar” / “hand drawn style”
- “turn your selfie into a cute doodle avatar”
- “sticker avatars”
- “childhood photo avatar”
- “Create a random image inspired by my X account”
- “Grok Imagine prompt:”
- “ChatGPT Images” / “sticker pack”

Exact product phrases such as `ai doodle avatar` remain low-volume in SEO and almost unused in these posts. Social copy should use the words people already type: **hand-drawn**, **doodle avatar**, **cartoon PFP**, **photo to sticker**.

## 4. What people are using right now

### A. ChatGPT Images — stickers (launched 2026-08-24)

OpenAI announced native sticker creation with transparent backgrounds and export to iMessage and WhatsApp. WhatsApp export requires three or more stickers. Coverage: [@ChatGPT](https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2091996384954069032), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/chatgpt-now-lets-users-create-custom-imessage-and-whatsapp-stickers/).

**Job:** turn a photo, idea, or inside joke into a messaging sticker pack.

**Doodle AI gap:** the live Sticker Pack skill produces a **square die-cut sticker sheet image**, not a transparent PNG pack with chat-app export. Do not advertise WhatsApp/iMessage stickers until that output exists.

### B. Grok Imagine

Public @grok replies route cartoon edits to Grok Imagine. Creators also use Imagine 1.5 to animate a still avatar into a short UGC reaction clip. One 2026-08-24 post claimed a workflow at about `$0.04` per selfie reaction video.

**Job:** generate or edit a cartoon in the same app where the user already is.

**Doodle AI implication:** on X, the competing “open this and make a cartoon” button is Grok, not a specialist site. Doodle AI wins only if the result looks more personal and more hand-drawn than a generic Imagine cartoon.

### C. LazyAvatar — [lazyavatar.com](https://lazyavatar.com)

This is the closest **language** competitor, not the closest **workflow** competitor.

- Positioning: “Hand-drawn avatars. PNG. One call.”
- Mechanism: seed-based API (`GET /v1/avatar/doodle?seed=hello&size=256`), not photo-to-cartoon.
- Styles: Classic Doodle, Punk & Alien, Big Head, Dog Doodles, Anime, Chibi Mascot.
- Price: `$4.99/month` or `$29.99/year`.
- On X, a builder pointed at it as the way to get the doodle-PFP look people were already seeing.

**Differentiation to keep honest:** Doodle AI turns **your photo** into a recognizable doodle. LazyAvatar generates a quirky doodle from a seed. Do not attack their API. Steal the clarity of “hand-drawn avatar / PNG / one action,” and keep photo likeness as the wedge.

### D. Other one-click doodle posts (2026-08-18 to 2026-08-24)

- [bo.video](https://bo.video/homeTry): “Turn your selfie into a cute doodle avatar.”
- aiai.com: “What would YOU look like as a hand-drawn avatar?”
- Tokenhot.ai: portrait → minimalist sticker avatars; “DM me STICKER for the exact prompt.”
- MyShell caricature generator: upload → generate → “drop yours.”

These posts are the current playbook: short video, one photo, one promise, one link.

## 6. ICP implications from live social behavior

These observations should update [icp.md](./icp.md). They do not replace it.

1. **The first customer is already collecting PFPs.** Cartoon PFP is used as identity and as a status signal. Some posts mock “doodle pfp” accounts; that is evidence of a recognizable look, not a reason to avoid it.
2. **Pet and couple demand is real in search, quieter in this X sample.** The live posts were mostly self-avatar, sticker, and prompt-sharing. Keep pet/couple pages, but do not assume X will pull those jobs the way profile pictures will.
3. **Stickers split into two jobs.** Messaging stickers (transparent, WhatsApp/iMessage) vs printable/die-cut sheets. ChatGPT now owns the first job. Doodle AI currently owns a version of the second.
4. **Creators pay for a method they can repeat.** JSON prompts, character refs, and “9 different vibes” posts are the creator ICP in the wild. Saved characters and @-mentions are the matching product feature; they are not marketed.
5. **Video is how stills travel.** People generate a still, then animate it in Grok Imagine. Doodle AI can supply the still without becoming a video product.

## 7. Content people would actually reshare

Do not start with a keyword essay. Start with posts that contain all four of:

1. A real Doodle AI output (original, not a competitor screenshot).
2. The input type (selfie, pet, no photo).
3. The exact user prompt or skill used.
4. A URL that performs the same workflow.

### Post types to ship first

| Post | Skill | Why it matches X |
|---|---|---|
| Selfie → one doodle PFP | `normal` | Directly copies the hand-drawn avatar trend |
| One selfie → six expressions | `collage` | Grid posts outperform single stills |
| One selfie → six full-body poses | `full-body` | Same grid mechanic; more distinctive |
| Photo → die-cut sticker sheet | `stickers` | Visual, but caption must say “sticker sheet,” not WhatsApp sticker |
| Photo → six captioned moods | `mood-captions` | Built for “share this” behavior |
| Occasion card from a photo | `gift` | Seasonal, but only with a real date and example |
| No-photo surprise character | `surprise` | Low friction for people who will not upload a face |

### What not to post

- Fake “best AI cartoon generator 2026” carousels.
- Claims of transparent WhatsApp stickers, print-ready files, or unlimited free generation.
- Competitor results labeled as Doodle AI.
- Seasonal/emotional-mode examples until those skills are runnable.

## 9. 30-day social execution

### Days 1–10 — proof

- Create the official X account and pin a one-sentence product fact: Doodle AI turns a photo into a hand-drawn doodle avatar at doodleai.art.
- Publish 9 original examples: 3 avatars, 2 collages, 2 sticker sheets, 1 mood-caption grid, 1 gift.
- Put those images on `/skills/normal/`, `/skills/collage/`, `/skills/stickers/`, and `/skills/mood-captions/` with alt text and captions.
- Fix public `llms.txt` so it matches the current product (see [aeo.md](./aeo.md)).

### Days 11–20 — method

- Each example post includes the prompt and the skill name.
- One “how I made this PFP” thread: photo → sign-in → skill → result.
- One honest comparison sentence: this is photo-likeness doodle art, not a seed-based avatar API and not a ChatGPT WhatsApp sticker.

### Days 21–30 — loop

- Ask people to quote with their result only after the workflow is reliable.
- Track: profile visits, link clicks, sign-ins, first successful generation, saves/downloads.
- Do not run a paid “AI cartoon generator” campaign until those events exist.

## 10. Metrics that match how people share

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Example posts published with real outputs | Without these, there is nothing to copy |
| Mentions of doodleai.art vs “Doodle AI” | Brand-name collision is real |
| Quote-posts and reply results | Evidence the look is being reused |
| Prompt-copy or skill-click from a post | Knowledge transfer, not vanity reach |
| Sign-in → first successful generation from social referrers | The only conversion that matches ICP activation |
| Second generation within 7 days | Whether the result was worth another try |
| Zero-tolerance: support tickets about WhatsApp stickers or API-key Settings copy | Detects message/product drift |
