# Doodle AI Marketing Research

This directory contains the customer, search, answer-engine, and social-distribution research for Doodle AI.

## Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [SEO research](./seo.md) | Live DataForSEO keyword volumes, competition, SERP formats, regional comparisons, content architecture, and a 90-day SEO plan. |
| [AEO and LLM visibility](./aeo.md) | Google AI Overview findings, People Also Ask questions, structured-content guidance, Grok measurement prompts, and LLM citation strategy. |
| [Ideal Customer Profile and revenue opportunities](./icp.md) | Priority customer segments, jobs-to-be-done, buying triggers, acquisition channels, revenue hypotheses, pricing experiments, and validation metrics. |
| [B2B studio and AI filmmaker research](./b2b.md) | Real market evidence, B2B account targets, paid-pilot offers, marketing channels, production functions, roadmap priorities, and commercial guardrails. |
| [Social knowledge-sharing](./social.md) | Live X posts on how people share cartoon/PFP/sticker workflows, prompt-copy behavior, adjacent tools, and the missing owned-channel plan. |

## Recommended reading order

1. Read [ICP](./icp.md) to understand who Doodle AI should serve and how it may earn revenue.
2. Read [Social](./social.md) to see how those customers actually discover, copy, and share visual tools on X.
3. Read [SEO](./seo.md) to select search-driven pages and keyword priorities.
4. Read [AEO](./aeo.md) to make those pages understandable and citeable by Google AI Overviews and language models.
5. Read [B2B](./b2b.md) only after the consumer workflow and public proof exist; it is a later revenue path, not the first launch motion.
